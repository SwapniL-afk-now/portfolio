Machine learning engineer & researcher

Ismam Nur Swapnil

I study how agents can learn, perceive, and reason with less data and computation.

I’m a Machine Learning Engineer in the Department of MIS at Advanced Chemical Industries Limited (ACI), where I work on Bangla OCR for handwritten and printed documents and Bangla text-to-speech. I collaborate with Prof. Ser-Nam Lim at the University of Central Florida on efficient policy optimization for language-model reasoning, and with Md Mofijul Islam at Amazon GenAI on on-policy self-distillation.

I earned a B.Sc. in Electrical & Electronic Engineering from BUET in 2025. My thesis studied AI-driven dermatological diagnosis with vision-language models, SFT, QLoRA, and DPO. I’m exploring PhD opportunities in machine learning.

Research

Learning from better signals

My research spans reinforcement learning, world models, visual reasoning, and on-policy distillation, with data and compute efficiency as a shared goal.

01

Efficient policy optimization for reasoning

With Prof. Ser-Nam Lim at UCF, I study efficient policy optimization for language-model reasoning. My first-author work PrePO extrapolates from observed same-batch updates; GRPO++ stabilizes GRPO for data-scarce medical VLMs.

02

On-policy distillation for reasoning

With Md Mofijul Islam at Amazon GenAI, I co-developed OPASD, which adds solution-conditioned attention supervision to on-policy self-distillation.

03

World models and visual reasoning

My independent research explores world models for reinforcement learning and sampling and inference-time scaling for language-model reasoning. Earlier work includes dermatological visual reasoning systems such as CLARIFY.
