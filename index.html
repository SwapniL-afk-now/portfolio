Research

Learning from better signals

I study how models can learn reliably from limited supervision, then run within real compute budgets.

01

Reinforcement learning for reasoning

I develop policy-gradient updates for reasoning models when rollouts are limited and rewards are noisy. GXPO uses gradient extrapolation to approximate a longer lookahead; GRPO++ adapts the training pipeline to low-resource medical VLMs.

02

Grounded, efficient vision-language models

I build smaller medical VLMs that can reason from images and external knowledge. CLARIFY pairs a specialist classifier with a compressed conversational model, while related work studies pruning and quantization.

03

Agents and practical multimodal systems

I explore graph-based long-video understanding, Bangla OCR, and domain-grounded assistants—systems designed to make useful decisions with limited compute and focused tools.
