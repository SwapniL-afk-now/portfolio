    Research directions

    Questions I want to pursue

    My work on optimization and distillation motivates two questions for future research.

    01

    When can we safely skip optimization work?

    PrePO studies prediction within repeated updates on a fixed batch. I’m interested in how prediction reliability and correction should adapt as training dynamics change.

    02

    Which learning signals make reasoning more efficient?

    OPASD transfers attention alongside token predictions; GRPO++ studies policy optimization with scarce medical data. I’m interested in how supervision and sampling affect the cost of learning useful reasoning.

    Ongoing interests. I’m also exploring world models for reinforcement learning and inference-time scaling for language-model reasoning.
